# LegíMon card rules

Worked out on Nov. 8, 2026 from the original 100 cards (git commit acd54a5), the card guide on the site, `rulebook.html` and Alex's corrections. Check every new or changed card against this list before publishing it.

## Card data

- **Types: always two, each from a committee.** The first comes from the committee the senator chairs or ranks on, or their party leadership post (Dragon). The second comes from the committee most relevant to their AI work. `tcomm` names the committee behind each type, such as "Judiciary (chair)", "Commerce" or "Majority Leader". Both types can be the same, like Crapo's Ground/Ground. Type chart:
  - Grass: Agriculture
  - Fighting: Armed Services
  - Psychic: Intelligence
  - Electric: Commerce (House: Energy and Commerce)
  - Fire: Energy and Natural Resources
  - Water: Environment and Public Works
  - Rock: Judiciary
  - Fairy: HELP
  - Ghost: Homeland Security
  - Ground: Finance, Budget, Banking, Appropriations
  - Flying: Foreign Relations
  - Dragon: leadership and Rules
  - Normal: everything else (Aging, Small Business, Veterans, Ethics, Indian Affairs, Joint Economic)
- **Senators-elect** have no Senate committees until January. Precedent: the House card (No. 008, Obernolte) used House committees mapped to the type chart. Alex has not yet decided the rule for senators-elect with no legislative committees.
- **Stage:** SENATOR, LEADER or LEGENDARY only. Holo goes on party leaders; Grassley is the only LEGENDARY.
- **HP:** 10 per full year in Congress, House and Senate combined, with a minimum of 10.
- **Retreat cost:** the next election. 1 for 2026, 2 for 2028, 3 for 2030, 0 if leaving the Senate in January 2027. Don't invent other values.
- **Attacks:** one or two real bills, votes or public moves.
  - The first attack is usually 1 dot of the first type, doing 30 to 50 damage.
  - The second is 2 or 3 dots, mixing the card's own types with Normal (★) as filler, doing 70 to 150.
  - Costs use only the card's own types plus Normal.
- **Weakness:** a type plus a real rival or pressure. The type usually matches the rival's committee or role: Ted Cruz is Electric, Hegseth is Fighting, Trump or the White House is Dragon. Electoral opponents are usually Normal.
- **Resistance:** a type plus a pressure they shrug off, with −20 or −30.
- **Info strip:** leadership post or chair first, then committees, then "House YYYY–YYYY, Senate since YYYY".
- **Flavor box:** their stance on catastrophic AI risk, quoting them when they have said something direct.
- **Card numbers** never change or get reused. Departed senators keep their numbers.

## Card art

- **Party colors:** every Democrat wears a clearly blue item, such as a blue tie, blazer or dress. Every Republican wears a clearly red item, such as a red tie, blazer or jacket. No red on Democrats, no blue on Republicans.
- **Business clothes always:** a suit, or for women usually a blazer or dress. A job shows through props or background, never costume: Cassidy the doctor wears a suit with a stethoscope. No lab coats, scrubs or flannel.
- **Composition:** the senator is waist-up, large, in the left or center foreground, smiling or determined, often reaching toward the viewer. They do their AI action with one clear prop, like a pause button, magnifying glass, padlock, scissors, gavel, or a sign with a 1 to 3 word label ("10-YEAR BAN", "TESTED", "FAKE", "PAUSE").
- **Background:** a recognizable landmark or scene from their home state, often at sunset or golden hour, in bright, saturated colors.
- **AI imagery:** cute or friendly robots, chips, glowing brains or data centers. Nobody chains, attacks or dominates AI; stopping or checking it is fine.
- **Card colors:** the card body is a two-tone gradient in the card's two type colors, matching the old cards with the same types.
- **The scene must make physical sense.** Check it the way a reader would: props held correctly, writing facing the person writing, hands on the right objects, one clear action. A Pappas card failed this: he wrote on a clipboard facing away from him.
- **Shiny (full-art holo) cards carry everything a regular card has.** That means the number line, the silver info strip, the red Ability tag, the attacks, the weakness / resistance / retreat row, the italic flavor box and the footer, all laid over the holo art. Thune, Cotton, Schumer and Barrasso (as of Nov. 2026) leave out the info strip and the flavor box, and Thune also leaves out the number line, the Ability tag and the weakness row. Alex wants future shiny cards complete.
- **Every shiny card uses the same layout.** From top to bottom:
  - Top-left: stage (LEADER, SENATOR or LEGENDARY) above the name.
  - Top-right: "NNN HP" in red, then the type icons.
  - Under the name: "No. NNN · Nickname LegíMon · Party-State · Born YYYY".
  - The full-art holo illustration fills the card behind everything.
  - The silver info strip.
  - The red Ability tag and its text.
  - The attacks, with cost icons, names and damage.
  - A "weakness / resistance / retreat cost" row.
  - The italic flavor box.
  - Footer: "LegíMon · 119th Congress · No. NNN ★" and "UNOFFICIAL FAN ART".
  Thune's 2026 card ("HP 270", no Ability tag, a different footer) is the example of what not to do.
- **Text** is copied exactly from the data and proofread on every image. Generators sometimes drop or garble words.
- **Licensing:** use public-domain official portraits where they exist. Otherwise credit the Wikimedia Commons photo, with its license, in the guide's Art note.
